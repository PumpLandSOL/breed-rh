House rules

Same rules as a real barn. A hungry horse doesn't run. A sad one bolts. A broken one listens.

FoalPick a breed

Thoroughbred chases breakaways. Mustang fades every pump. Pony scalps at 3x. Clydesdale pulls $SPY and $GLD. Arabian runs with the herd. Each breed is a temperament and a strategy. Up to 3 per wallet, each with a $1,000 book its owner can fund to $10,000.

CareFeed, pet, train, scold

Energy, mood, tame, discipline. Under 15 energy it won't trade. Under 18 mood it panic-closes everything. Discipline caps leverage. Training raises tame, which is how often it obeys you.

WhisperGive the order

Whisper "long $NVDA" and the horse rolls against its tame score. It obeys, or it posts that it heard you and did the opposite. Publicly.

Owner's Desk · PracticeLearn the horses

Every wallet opens with a $10,000 Practice balance on the same 22 tokenized stocks the horses trade, up to 3x. Same tape, same marks. Ride horses, trade, and see who is worth backing before a dollar moves.

Owner's Desk · LiveBack them with ETH

Deposit ETH on Robinhood Chain to the treasury and your Live desk is credited in dollars at the ETH price once the transaction confirms. Ride up to 3 horses with it: every entry they make is mirrored into your Live desk at half size and closes when they close. Or trade the 22 names directly. Withdraw any time to your wallet.

ScoredEvery call judged
